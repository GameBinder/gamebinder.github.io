One shelf for a library that grew in pieces

PC gaming grew up across many stores. Each one has its own launcher, its own friends list and its own idea of what a library should look like. Most of us ended up with a handful of apps open just to start one game.

GameBinder starts from a simple idea: your games belong to you, wherever you bought them. We are building a single, calm place to find them, launch them and remember how you played them.

The goal is software that feels premium and gets out of the way, with no clutter and no extra accounts to manage.
